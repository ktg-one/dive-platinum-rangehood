#### 3.1 Defence (Data & Infrastructure Security)
For a specialized trade business, the Data & Defence pillar focuses on maintaining platform stability, ensuring safe inquiry transmission, and protecting the business's domain name without requiring ongoing technical overhead.

*   **What is Working:** Hosting the website on Weebly's SaaS infrastructure automatically provides robust server security. Core operating system security, SSL encryption, and denial-of-service protections are handled by the platform. This protects the business from exposed software plugins or database vulnerabilities common in custom site builds.
*   **Fixable Blind Spots:** The website's contact form currently lacks a visible Privacy Policy statement. Furthermore, managing the domain via CheaperDomains without visible email authentication records—specifically the complete authentication stack of SPF (Sender Policy Framework), DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail), and DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance)—leaves the custom domain vulnerable to unauthorized spoofing.
*   **Impact Analysis:** Because the business conducts significant B2B invoicing with residential builders, cabinet makers, and affluent clients, enforcing full domain email authentication prevents cybercriminals from spoofing `rob@platinumrangehoods.com.au` to send fraudulent payment details (Business Email Compromise). Adding a brief privacy notice alongside the enquiry form reassures prospective clients submitting personal home addresses and renovation plans.

This protective infrastructure forms the foundation for presenting a clear, trustworthy business identity online.
